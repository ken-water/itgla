Refund Policy

ITGLA is currently free during early access and does not accept payments on itgla.com.

The current Windows and Linux releases can be downloaded without an account, subscription, or purchase. Because ITGLA does not currently charge you, there is no payment to cancel or refund.

Mandatory rights

Nothing in this policy limits refund, cancellation, cooling-off, warranty, or other consumer rights that cannot be excluded under applicable law.

Questions

For a billing or refund question, use the ITGLA GitHub repository to request a private contact route. Do not post payment details or other sensitive information publicly.
