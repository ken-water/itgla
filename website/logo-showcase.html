A clear mark for a connected workspace.

Six directions built around ITGLA’s promise: make the relationships between servers, domains, services, and projects easy to see and easy to use.

Local-firstResource graphCalm, technical, trustworthyWorks at favicon scale
01 / NODE CORE

Shared center of gravity

A direct evolution of the current node map. The center is the project or operator; the surrounding nodes are the resources it depends on.

Best for: primary product mark
02 / FOLDED PATH

One route through complexity

Two paths fold into a single route, suggesting a practical workflow from discovery to action without visual noise.

Best for: app icon and motion
03 / GRID GATEWAY

Structure with a way through

A quiet inventory grid with a chevron gateway. It communicates organization first, with movement and access built in.

Best for: enterprise-facing identity
04 / ORBIT STACK

Systems in relationship

Three orbital planes turn the relationship graph into a compact, memorable symbol with a stronger systems feel.

Best for: graph view and launch screen
05 / ITGLA I

An unmistakable initial

A confident I built from a bridge and two endpoints. It is the most legible option at tiny sizes and in monochrome.

Best for: favicon and installer
06 / PRISM LINKS

Many layers, one view

A contained prism frames the links between layers of infrastructure, matching ITGLA’s promise of clarity across a growing stack.

Best for: website hero and campaigns
Recommendation: start with Node Core for continuity, or ITGLA I for the strongest small-size system. Choose one direction before replacing the production favicon and website mark.
