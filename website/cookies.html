Cookie Policy

This page describes cookies and similar browser storage used by itgla.com.

Public website

The public product, download, feedback, and policy pages do not set analytics, advertising, preference, or personalization cookies. Public traffic measurement is performed from minimized server request logs rather than browser tracking scripts.

Strictly necessary administrator cookie

CookiePurposeDurationAccess
itgla_admin_sessionAuthenticates the restricted site analytics administrator.Up to 8 hours, or until logoutSecure, HttpOnly, SameSite=Strict, limited to /admin

Consent and controls

Because the public site uses no non-essential cookies, it does not display a cookie-consent banner. The administrator session cookie is strictly necessary for the requested sign-in function. Authorized administrators can remove it by signing out or clearing site data in the browser.

Server logs are not cookies

The hosting server processes limited request information even when cookies are disabled. See the Privacy Policy for categories, purposes, minimization, and retention.

Changes and contact

If non-essential cookies are introduced, this policy and any required consent controls will be updated before they are enabled. Questions can be raised through the ITGLA GitHub repository without including sensitive information.
