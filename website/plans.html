What stays free

Basic local ownership, export, backup, recovery and safety fixes stay free. ITGLA will not require a subscription to retrieve your local inventory. The customer integration API is intended for Pro; internal website and admin endpoints are separate.

Full database backups contain sensitive data and are not encrypted containers. The 0.6.3 handover summary omits credentials and notes, but names, addresses and contacts can still be sensitive.
