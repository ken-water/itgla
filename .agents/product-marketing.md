# ITGLA product context

Updated: 2026-10-29.

Brand idea: **IT in a glance.** Help users understand what IT resources they have, which business they support, how they connect, who is responsible, and what must be passed on.

Primary situations: day-to-day management, staff changes, server migrations, service handovers, company sales, and technology transfers. Start with independent developers, small IT operators and technical service providers; enterprise MSP feature requirements are not automatically in scope.

Current desktop foundations include local server/service/domain/business records, tags/notes, directed relationships, connection-map views, verification metadata and operation history. The local phase-one preview adds optional technical/backup contacts, environment, documentation references, information source and manual checks to existing entities. Formal handover acceptance remains planned; this preview is not yet publicly released. Do not market proposed workflows as shipping features.

Product direction and asset taxonomy: `docs/it-in-a-glance-product-direction.md`. This is a phased proposal. Near-term focus: easy maintenance, responsibility/context, business-level views, complete recovery and offline handover information. Formal handover confirmation, broader account/vendor/code asset models and remote collaboration need later validation and implementation.

Current download is free. Existing $49/year Founder Pro roadmap pricing is a hypothesis, not measured willingness to pay or a claim of paying customers. Market research identified strong free alternatives and demand for portable, recoverable documentation. Keep observations, interpretations and purchase evidence separate.

Website: itgla.com. Public copy remains English. The website should clearly express the brand idea and practical use cases, without implying automatic discovery, uptime monitoring, cloud sync, legal transfer automation or a complete secrets vault.

## Free-first decision (2026-11-06)

The owner selected three tiers: Free, Pro and Team, with useful free activation and retention first. Basic local ownership, search, relationships, manual reminders, export, backup and recovery belong to Free. API/automation and optional cloud continuity are planned for Pro; shared workspaces, permissions and team audit are planned for Team. No paid pricing or availability has been approved. Follow docs/free-first-delivery-20261106.md; earlier Founder Pro prices are historical hypotheses. Local candidate improvements are not included in existing 0.6.2 downloads. Do not claim real user validation, closed-app reminders or paid API availability.
