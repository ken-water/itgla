## Summary

See your IT environment at a glance. ITGLA connects domains, servers, services and businesses so you can find what runs where, understand dependencies, and hand over infrastructure with less guesswork.

## Description

ITGLA is a local-first IT asset inventory for independent developers and small teams.

Record the assets that matter:

- servers and hosting details
- services, ports, versions and access notes
- domains, subdomains and expiry dates
- businesses, owners, tags and handover notes
- relationships such as runs on, deployed to, depends on, resolves to and backs up to

Use search, tables and a relationship map to answer practical questions quickly:

- Which server runs this domain?
- What services are on this host?
- What depends on this database?
- Who owns this asset and what needs attention?

Your workspace stays local on your device. Export and backup support handovers and recovery. Keep passwords and private keys in a dedicated password manager; ITGLA stores references and operational context.
